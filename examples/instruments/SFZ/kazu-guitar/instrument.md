# Kazu Guitar — SFZ Instrument

This instrument uses the same six genuine recorded guitar samples as the
`sounds/soundpiano/kazu-guitar` SoundPiano pack, with no duplicate audio files.
Keep the two folders together under the same NoteScript root.

```notescript-instrument
name: Kazu Guitar (SFZ)
version: 1
author: Kazu (original guitar recordings)
license: Distributed with NoteScript by recording-owner permission
source: Original GR-01.wav through GR-06.wav recorded by the contributor
description: Six-zone guitar-string SFZ mapped to E-flat standard tuning.
format: SFZ
entry: kazu-guitar.sfz
```
