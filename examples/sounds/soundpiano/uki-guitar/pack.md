# Uki Guitar — Original Guitar Recordings

Six original notes recorded by the user. The source recordings are preserved unchanged at their original location; the playable copies are downsampled from 192 kHz PCM32 stereo to 48 kHz PCM16 stereo for the portable library.

The actual audio is close to standard guitar tuning (E-A-D-G-B-E), **not E-flat tuning**. No retuning is applied. The unchanged 192 kHz / 32-bit Stereo original files are in `masters/` for offline HQ Stereo WAV rendering; the compact samples in this folder remain optimized for the Piano. For provenance and reuse terms, see `docs/development/reports/UKI-GUITAR-RECORDINGS.md`.
